I am an Assistant Professor in the [Grado Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://www.ise.vt.edu/) at [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/). Previously, I was an AI Research Scientist at [Meta](https://ai.meta.com/research/) and a postdoctoral associate at the [University of Minnesota](https://twin-cities.umn.edu/) ([ECE](https://cse.umn.edu/ece)), mentored by [Mingyi Hong](https://people.ece.umn.edu/~mhong/mingyi.html) and [Shuzhong Zhang](https://sites.google.com/umn.edu/shuzhong-zhang). I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from [UC Davis](https://www.ucdavis.edu/) ([Mathematics](https://math.ucdavis.edu/)), advised by [Krishna Balasubramanian](https://sites.google.com/view/kriznakumar/) and [Shiqian Ma](https://engineering.jhu.edu/ams/faculty/shiqian-ma/), and my B.S. in Mathematics from [Zhejiang University](http://www.zju.edu.cn/english/).

My research bridges optimization, operations research, and AI. I develop mathematical foundations and algorithms for efficient foundation-model training and agentic AI alignment, while exploring how AI can advance optimization and applied mathematics.

<strong class="recruiting-heading">Fully funded Ph.D. openings — Fall 2027.</strong> I am recruiting **1–2 Ph.D. students** to work with me in these areas. Applicants interested in related areas of theoretical or applied AI are also welcome. Our group actively collaborates with researchers at **Meta, Amazon, and Microsoft**, creating opportunities for joint research and large-scale experimentation. Please [email me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}) your CV and research interests.
